Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Expense Tracker.

How do I add a new expense?

Click the "Add Expense" button in the sidebar or any "+ Add New Expense" button, fill in the title, amount, category, and date, then click Save.

Where is my data stored?

Your transactions are stored in your secure backend server and mirrored locally in your browser storage so you can always access your records offline.

How does the budget limit work?

You can set a monthly spending budget in Settings > Expense Preferences. Your monthly spending progress will be monitored against this goal.

Can I export my expense data?

Yes! Under Settings > Data Management, click "Export as CSV" or "Download Reports" to download your full spending history anytime.
